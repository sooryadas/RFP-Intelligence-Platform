## Q1 — Bid1
**Question:** What is the revised submission deadline for the computing devices RFP?

**Answer:** The revised submission deadline for the Student and Staff Computing Devices RFP is July 9, 2024 at 2:00 PM CST

**Sources:**
- Addendum 2 RFP JA-207652 Student and Staff Computing Devices.pdf, page 1

## Q2 — Bid1
**Question:** What minimum warranty is required for student Chromebooks?

**Answer:** The District requires a minimum one‑year warranty on all student Chromebooks

**Sources:**
- Addendum 1 RFP JA-207652 Student and Staff Computing Devices.pdf, page 3
- JA-207652 Student and Staff Computing Devices FINAL.pdf, page 4

## Q3 — Bid1
**Question:** What warranty is required for student and staff Windows laptops?

**Answer:** The district requires a minimum three‑year warranty for all Windows laptops, whether they are for students or for staff

**Sources:**
- Addendum 1 RFP JA-207652 Student and Staff Computing Devices.pdf, page 3

## Q4 — Bid1
**Question:** How should the awarded vendor coordinate deliveries?

**Answer:** The awarded vendor must work with Dallas ISD’s Ed Tech department to set up a delivery schedule. In other words, the vendor “will need to coordinate a schedule of deliveries with the Dallas ISD ED TECH department.

**Sources:**
- Addendum 1 RFP JA-207652 Student and Staff Computing Devices.pdf, page 4
- Addendum 1 RFP JA-207652 Student and Staff Computing Devices.pdf, page 2

## Q5 — Bid1
**Question:** Does Dallas ISD require vendors to maintain inventory in stock?

**Answer:** No. Dallas ISD does not require vendors to keep inventory in stock. The district’s addendum states, “Dallas ISD is not requiring vendors to keep inventory in stock. The District asks that the awarded vendor meet the deadline for the agreed delivery.”

**Sources:**
- Addendum 1 RFP JA-207652 Student and Staff Computing Devices.pdf, page 2

## Q9 — Bid2
**Question:** How must vendors submit their PORFP responses?

**Answer:** Vendors may submit their Purchase Order Request for Proposal (PORFP) responses only through the State’s eMaryland Marketplace Advantage (eMMA) e‑procurement system. Bids are not accepted by email, fax, U.S. Mail, or hand‑delivery; vendors must be registered and logged in to eMMA to upload their proposals.
